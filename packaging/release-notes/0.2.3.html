Splashboard 0.2.3

  • Remove data no longer deletes models or a cache you chose to keep when they sit in a folder inside the data folder.
  • Quitting during a storage move no longer leaves your models in one place and the setting pointing at another. A move cut short by a crash or power loss is finished or undone at the next start.
  • Chat no longer crashes with models that use more than one chat template.
  • Opening another conversation while a reply streams no longer shows that reply in the wrong conversation, and edits made just before switching are saved.
  • Deleting a chat no longer removes an attachment another chat has just uploaded, and a chat deleted while it replies stays deleted.
  • A request cancelled before the engine answered no longer leaves the engine marked busy, which blocked model switching and idle unloading.

Engine: Splash by inco.ai, 1.3.0 or newer (Homebrew incoai/tap/splash). Splashboard is not an inco.ai product.

Splashboard is not notarized. On a first install, open it once from System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
